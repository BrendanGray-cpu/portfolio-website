Hey, I’m Brendan.

I’m a product designer in Salt Lake City. I started in graphic design, moved into B2B product work, and now I prototype nearly everything in code.

My BFA is in graphic design from the University of Utah, with a minor in strategic communications. My first UX job was at the university library, where I ran user tests on its web pages.

At Graphite Connect I designed a network procurement platform, helping the small startup rapidly grow in employee and user count. At BambooHR I worked on a few teams in the Growth portfolio, focusing on transactions, new acquisitions and checkouts.

I’m currently working as a freelance designer, helping Graphite Connect with its workflow editors. I’m looking for my next product design role.
