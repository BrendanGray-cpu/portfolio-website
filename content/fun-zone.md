## Motorcycle Engines
tag: Three.js
image: previews/motorcycle-engines.webp
clip: fun-engine.mp4
alt: A 3D motorcycle engine, exploded into its parts.
link: https://motorcycle-engine.vercel.app/
what: A 3D engine you can rotate, explode into parts and slice open, with a guided tour of where the energy goes.
more: I ride, and I wanted to understand my bike well enough to do real work on it. It was my first Three.js project, and each round I asked how it could teach a little more.

## Wellspring Group
tag: Study
image: previews/wellspring-group.webp
clip: fun-wellspring.mp4
alt: A brand site for a fictional beverage company, with floating bubbles.
link: https://wellspring-group.vercel.app/
what: A brand site for a made-up beverage company, rebuilt from a brand site I admire with my own company, logo, colors and copy.
more: Most marketing sites feel the same, and that one didn’t. Rebuilding its bubble physics taught me timing, layout and color at once. Copying work I admire is one of the fastest ways I learn.

## Vantage Financial
tag: Dashboard
image: previews/vantage-financial.webp
clip: fun-vantage.mp4
alt: A trading dashboard with holdings, news and a stock chart.
link: https://vantage-financial-five.vercel.app/
what: A trading dashboard with holdings, market news, stock data and trades.
more: A dense dashboard you can customize and resize is a good test of system design. I built it on Apple’s Human Interface Guidelines and designed every state each panel can be in.
