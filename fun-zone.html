Practice

Taste Exercises

A practice for training my eye. I pick a small piece of great work, rebuild it pixel for pixel, then write down why it works.

Measuring is where I notice things, and writing is where I find out how to understand them. Each exercise ends with a statement about the design or a rule I didn’t know before I started. Each slide shows the original next to my rebuild.

Study

Wellspring Group

A brand site for a made-up beverage company, rebuilt from a brand site I admire with my own company, logo, colors and copy.

Most marketing sites feel the same, and that one didn’t. Rebuilding its bubble physics taught me timing, layout and color at once. Copying work I admire is one of the fastest ways I learn.
