Product builder

I’m a product designer who started in graphic design. Since then I’ve taken on product management work, designed B2B software for almost five years, and started prototyping nearly everything in code.

I work on complex software with strict requirements, helping them grow and scale: procurement, HR, billing, contracts. My goal is a solution that feels obvious to the person using it.

AI has changed how I work more than anything else in my career. I use it to test ideas in real code within days, and it helps me learn and explore in ways I never could before.
